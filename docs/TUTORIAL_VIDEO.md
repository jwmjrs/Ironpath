# Ironpath Feature Tour

This is a captions-first walkthrough for the public test site. It is designed
to run for roughly four to five minutes and can be recorded without narration.

## Recording setup

- Use the live test site in a desktop-sized browser window.
- Start at the landing page with the Dark theme selected.
- Use an existing connected group if one is available. If not, show the group
  connection flow without entering private information on screen.
- Keep each interaction deliberate: one click, a short pause, then the next.

## Scene 1 — Welcome to Ironpath (0:00–0:20)

**Show:** The landing page, logo, short project introduction, and the Enter
Ironpath button.

**On-screen caption:**

> Ironpath is a shared RuneScape 3 companion for Ironman and Group Ironman
> accounts.

**Optional narration:**

> Ironpath keeps useful group data, account planning, daily activities, and
> practical reference tools in one place.

**Action:** Select **Enter Ironpath**.

## Scene 2 — Settings and group connection (0:20–0:45)

**Show:** Open Settings from the landing page, then show the group lookup and
refresh controls.

**On-screen caption:**

> Connect your Group Ironman team once. Ironpath uses that selection across
> the dashboard, progression tools, and recommendations.

**Optional narration:**

> Settings is the home for group lookup, refreshing public data, and quest
> access syncing. Your selected player also carries across the site wherever
> personal levels matter.

## Scene 3 — Dashboard (0:45–1:25)

**Show:** Dashboard with Member standings, Adventurer’s log, and Drop archive.
Open one member’s stats, then close it. Highlight the group key area without
showing a real private key.

**On-screen caption:**

> See the team at a glance: standings, recent activity, and shared item
> history.

**Optional narration:**

> The Dashboard is the group’s quick reference point. Member standings show
> the current roster and skill progress. The Adventurer’s log highlights recent
> public activity, while the Drop archive helps the group keep track of
> meaningful finds together.

## Scene 4 — Progression roadmap (1:25–2:00)

**Show:** Progression, then Roadmap. Click a Route phase, mark one checklist
item, and point out the subtle Sync quests control.

**On-screen caption:**

> Follow a practical Ironman roadmap, one phase and one checklist item at a
> time.

**Optional narration:**

> The Roadmap breaks a long-term account plan into manageable phases. Each
> step can be checked off manually, and completed quest steps can be synced
> from the selected player when public quest data is available.

## Scene 5 — Training and unlocks (2:00–2:35)

**Show:** Skill Training, choose a skill and selected member. Then open
Unlocks, switch between two categories, expand a card, and mark an item done.

**On-screen caption:**

> Match training methods and useful unlocks to the account you are playing.

**Optional narration:**

> Skill Training gives level-based method ideas for the selected player.
> Unlocks turns useful account upgrades, travel options, activities, and gear
> goals into an organized personal checklist.

## Scene 6 — Distractions and Diversions (2:35–3:05)

**Show:** Distractions and Diversions. Demonstrate checking a circle, hovering
over an activity title for its details, and opening Activity Settings.

**On-screen caption:**

> Keep dailies, weeklies, and longer-term activities in one clear cycle.

**Optional narration:**

> Only the circle completes an activity. Selecting the activity itself reveals
> helpful context, so you can decide whether it is worth doing before marking
> it off. Activity Settings lets you hide tasks you do not want in your own
> routine.

## Scene 7 — Utility: Shop Runs and Routes (3:05–3:45)

**Show:** Utility → Shop Runs. Mark one shop category. Then open Routes,
switch between Herb, Tree, and Fruit tree cards, select a patch, and enter a
short note.

**On-screen caption:**

> Build simple routines around the supplies and routes that matter to an
> Ironman account.

**Optional narration:**

> Shop Runs organizes common Ironman supply stops by category, location, and
> stock to keep. Routes provides a compact farming circuit with recommended
> crop guidance based on the selected player’s Farming level, patch travel
> reminders, individual completion circles, and device-local notes.

## Scene 8 — Utility: reference and test tools (3:45–4:25)

**Show:** Archaeology Collections, open a collector and check one artefact.
Then show Dungeon Tools and select a boss. Finish by opening Task Generator.

**On-screen caption:**

> Reference tools turn research-heavy activities into practical checklists and
> quick lookups.

**Optional narration:**

> Archaeology Collections keeps collectors and artefacts organized as you work
> through them. Dungeon Tools brings together Daemonheim supplies and boss
> reference information. Task Generator is an experimental feature for finding
> a useful next objective.

## Scene 9 — Closing (4:25–4:45)

**Show:** Return to the Dashboard, then the footer links for About, FAQ, and
Privacy & data.

**On-screen caption:**

> Ironpath is built by players, shaped by practical use, and still growing with
> community feedback.

**Optional narration:**

> Ironpath began as a passion project for Justjay and a Group Ironman team.
> Feedback from real play continues to guide what is added, changed, and made
> easier to use.

## End card

**Show:** Ironpath landing page logo.

**Caption:**

> Plan. Track. Progress. Together.
