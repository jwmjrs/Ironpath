# Ironpath quick start

This guide gets a Group Ironman team using Ironpath in a few minutes.

## 1. Connect your group

1. Open **Dashboard** from the header.
2. Enter your Group Ironman group’s exact official name.
3. Refresh the group data.

Ironpath reads public RuneScape-facing information. If a group or character cannot be found, first confirm the spelling and public RuneMetrics/Adventurer’s Log visibility.

## 2. Choose your character

On the Dashboard, use **View as** to choose yourself. Ironpath remembers that choice across relevant pages.

This personalises useful information such as:

- Familiar options available at your Summoning level.
- Farming recommendations for your Farming level.
- Skill-training references.
- Repeatable activities and their known access requirements.

## 3. Plan your next session

Choose the section that fits what you are doing:

| If you want to… | Open… |
| --- | --- |
| Check a group member’s public progress | Dashboard |
| Clear today’s routine | Distractions and Diversions |
| Find one productive objective | Task Generator |
| Follow a herb, tree, or fruit-tree route | Supplemental Info → Farming Routes |
| Check a skill, unlock, or progression reference | Resources |

## 4. Use the group item history when needed

The Dashboard’s **Group item history** lets the team search known drops and item milestones. It defaults to all members so it remains a group record.

If your group wants to share this history across browsers or devices, use the **drop archive key** section. One person copies the key; other members choose **Join archive** and paste it into the in-app prompt.

The key is only for shared item-history data. It is not a RuneScape password or account-login method.

## 5. Keep expectations realistic

Public activity can be delayed or unavailable. A missing activity does not necessarily mean someone’s character is configured incorrectly or that Ironpath has lost data.

Common reasons are:

- The public profile is private or uses a differently formatted display name.
- RuneScape has not surfaced the event in its public activity feed.
- A public endpoint is temporarily rate-limited or unavailable.

For a complete overview of every section, see the [feature guide](features.md).
